Solutions · Accounts payable

When the maker-checker is an AI agent

AP teams are handing invoice runs to agents. Magertron™ keeps the controls finance already relies on: a second person for risky payments, and no one approving an invoice on their own.

The risk

Business email no longer can compromise just a person.

A vendor email says the bank details changed. A person might call to check. An agent with access to the payables system just pays.

Magertron checks each payment the agent makes against the company's rules. Routine invoices go through. A payment to a new bank account, or over the limit, waits for the AP supervisor.

What does the work

The controls

  • Acting for a person. The agent acts for Joe through a signed token exchange, and every call records both.
  • Payment rules. Approved payees and a per-account limit, in policy data your finance team controls.
  • Holds for a named approver. Named supervisors decide. They need no access to the AP system itself.
  • No self-approval. Maker-checker applies to the agent and to the person behind it.
  • One approval, one payment. An approval releases that payment once. A second identical call is refused.
  • One trace. Every call, hold and decision, in OpenTelemetry.

Questions

Frequently asked

▸Does this replace the approvals in our ERP or AP platform?

No. It adds them where the agent acts: at the API call. Your AP platform's own approval flow still applies to people using it directly.

▸Who can approve a held payment?

The people named as approvers in the policy data, for example every AP supervisor. They decide in Magertron and need no access to the AP system.

▸Can one approval pay twice?

No. An approval releases exactly that payment, once. A second identical call is refused.
