How it works

Four commands, empty folder to governed app.

Czartup™ keeps the whole app in one short document, the Czarter. Every command reads it, so the IDE, the terminal and the Magertron™ console always agree.

/czartup-new

Describe it

Say what the app does, who uses it, its limits and approvals. Czartup drafts the Czarter and saves nothing until you agree.

/czartup-setup

Set it up

A preview of every change, then one yes: the IdP app with MFA, the agent's identity, people, policy and grants.

/czartup-build

Build it

Your coding assistant builds the servers and the app from Czartup's hand-over pack, then stops for the administrator.

/czartup-verify

Prove it

Live checks against the platform: limits refuse, holds wait, the agent reaches only its own tools. Run it in CI too.

Also: /czartup-approvals builds the app's approvals screen, /czartup-update follows a changed Czarter, /czartup-status says what's next, and /czartup-retire removes an app cleanly.

What you get

The governance you'd otherwise hand-code.

None of it lives in the app. Every call goes through Magertron™, so the controls hold even if the app is wrong or compromised.

Sign-in with MFA

The app is created in your identity provider, with MFA for this app only. Other apps on the tenant are untouched.

Two identities on every call

The agent and the person it acts for, joined at Magertron's STS. Policy and audit see both.

Limits

"A fuel sale is at most $500" becomes a policy rule in OPA. A call that breaks it is refused at the gateway.

Approvals

Sensitive calls wait for a named person. The one who asked can never approve it, and one approval lets exactly one call through.

An agent in its lane

When the nightly agent runs with nobody signed in, it may call only the tools you listed. Everything else is refused.

Audit, from the first call

Every call, refusal and approval is recorded to the agent and the person. Retire an app and it leaves no grants behind.

Built for the platform team

Developers move fast. Administrators keep the keys.

Czartup™ acts as the person signed in, with their own Magertron™ roles. Setting an app up needs an administrator, and every change is shown as a preview first.

  • An administrator decides which new servers the gateway trusts
  • Each app gets its own namespace, role and policy; it can't touch another app's rules
  • Secrets go straight to Vault, never into a file or the chat
  • Every change is audited to the person who said yes

Get started

Install the CLI and sign in.

Czartup™ runs inside your Magertron™. Install the CLI on any machine, sign in through your browser, and add the slash commands to your IDEs.

1. Install

On Debian or Ubuntu it installs with apt, so updates come with the rest of your system.

curl -fsSL https://magertron.com/install-czartup.sh | sh

2. Sign in and add the commands

Use the address you open the Magertron™ console or developer portal at.

czartup login https://your-magertron czartup commands

3. Describe your first app

Open an empty folder in your IDE and type /czartup-new followed by what the app does, who uses it, and its limits and approvals.

/czartup-new Use Czartup to build ... on Magertron, in the namespace ...
