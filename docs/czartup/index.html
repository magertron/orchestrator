Czartup Preview

Magertron Czartup™

Declare a governed agent or app once. Czartup™ configures everything and delivers to your coding assistant to build and maintain.

1 · Describe A Czarter: who signs in, the agent, its tools, limits and holds.
2 · Set up Czartup configures sign-in, roles, the agent and its grants.
3 · Hand over A build pack in formats coding agents already read.
4 · Build Say “Build” in Cursor, Claude Code, Devin or VS Code.
5 · Proven The Czarter's checks run as live calls through the gateway.

One document says what the app may do

The Czarter. Written by whoever is accountable for the app — the customer's admin or the SI.

PeopleWho signs in, with which identity provider, and whether MFA and a fresh login are required.
The agentThe service account it acts as, when it runs, what it may and must never do.
ConnectionsThe MCP servers, APIs and models it reaches — and exactly which tools.
ControlsLimits that refuse a call and holds that wait for a person's approval.
