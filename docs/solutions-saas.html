Magertron™ AgentSaaS

The control plane for AI SaaSGovern your estate from one place

Magertron™ AI Orchestrator runs in your cluster, between your agents and ServiceNow, Salesforce, Workday and your own systems. One policy, one audit trail, every vendor’s usage metered.

Three kinds of call

What happens depends on who is calling and where the call goes.

your agent → ServiceNow

Agents you built, on ServiceNow's MCP server

  • Every call carries the agent and the person it acts for.
  • Your rules apply on top of ServiceNow's: closing a P1 waits for the on-call engineer.
  • No lock-in to Agent Studio: the same agent also works on Salesforce and Workday.
your agent → Salesforce

Agents on a platform that bills per action

  • Every call into Salesforce is counted and priced against your contract.
  • Caps by team, user or agent stop a looping agent before the invoice does.
  • Deal-desk rules: discounts over a limit wait for the manager.
SaaS agent → your systems

Vendor agents reaching inside

  • Your ERP, billing and data are registered in Magertron as MCP or REST servers.
  • An Agentforce or Now Assist agent calling them gets the same identity, policy and audit as your own agents.

Why neutral matters

The referee isn't a player

A company doesn't want ServiceNow deciding what agents may do in Salesforce, or the reverse. One neutral layer applies the company's own rules to both.

Agents stay portable

Built against Magertron, an agent reaches ServiceNow, Salesforce and internal systems the same way. Switching or adding a vendor doesn't mean rebuilding governance.

Metered platforms need a meter

When SaaS bills per agent action, finance needs every vendor's usage in one place, rated against contracts and capped before month-end.
