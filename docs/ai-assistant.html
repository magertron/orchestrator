Magertron™ AI Assistant

Ask your AI estate anythingThen let it handle the busywork, with a person deciding

The assistant built into the Magertron™ console answers questions about your MCP servers, models, agents and spend from the live platform, not from a guess. It finds what needs attention, walks admins through setup, and drafts the next step. It is advisory and read-only: it explains and suggests; a person decides.

What it does for you

?

Answers from live data

Busiest server, most expensive model, what's over budget, which agents call tools without a grant. Answers come from the platform's own state, with the names and numbers to act on.

⬡

Guided server setup

Walks an admin through adding an MCP server: asks the questions in order, keeps a snapshot of the answers as it goes, and hands back a configuration to review.

◷

A daily estate digest

Findings land in the assistant rail without anyone asking: servers that are dark or under-used against what their contract costs, average cost per prompt, and what changed.

⚖

Policy suggestions

Rates every tool on your live servers by what it can do, flags money-moving and data tools that no rule covers, and drafts a starter policy for a person to review. It never writes policy itself.

$

Spend in plain English

"What's our average cost per prompt?" "Who is near their allowance?" The assistant answers from the same metering and chargeback data finance uses.

⚑

Security red flags

Ungranted access, agents calling alone where a person is required, tools whose definitions changed. Surfaced with who, what and how often, so the review starts with facts.

Built to be trusted

  • Advisory and read-only. It reads the platform; it doesn't change it. Nothing happens until a person acts.
  • Grounded. Answers come from a snapshot of your live platform state, not from the model's memory.
  • In your cluster. It runs inside your Magertron™ install, with the same governance as every other model call.
